fix: a confirmation must have an attempt to refute it, and the report splits by target

Two problems found by reading a LIVE /loop run on Checkmk (12 rounds, 31
hypotheses, 43 minutes):

  rounds: {recon: 1, generate: 10, coverage: 1}     ← verify: 0
  tree:   31 hypotheses  {pending: 23, confirmed: 7, blocked: 1}
  confirmed severities: {info: 3, high: 2, medium: 2}

(1) THE FALSIFICATION CHECK WAS INERT.

All 7 confirmed findings were REPRODUCED tier — real commands against a real
target — but `verification_recorded: 0`. Not one probe had ever run: the model read
code, ran a command via bash, and recorded `confirmed` with hand-supplied evidence.
So the check that refuses a `confirmed` verdict over a FALSIFIED probe could never
fire, and every confirmation had nothing behind it but the auditor's own word.

- `Hypothesis.refutation` — what the auditor tried in order to prove the finding
  WRONG, and why it held.
- `hypothesis_record` now REFUSES `confirmed` unless there is a survived
  verification run OR a written refutation attempt, and the refusal names both ways
  out. An empty attempt is normalised away: recording one would satisfy the gate
  without saying anything, which is worse than not having the gate.
- It runs AFTER the evidence check, so a call with no evidence still fails on that
  — the more fundamental problem — instead of being hidden behind a message about
  refutation.
- The written attempt is printed in the report as the weaker evidence it is:
  "Refutation attempt (written): …", and a confirmation with neither says
  "Refutation attempt: NONE — it is the auditor agreeing with itself".

(2) A REPORT FOR A "PRE-AUTH HIGH" GOAL READ AS THOUGH THE TARGET WAS MET.

7 confirmed findings of which 3 were `info`, all in one list.

- The confirmed section is split at the contract's `minSeverity`, defaulting to
  `medium` when there is no contract:
    ## 已确认发现（达标：≥ high，1 条）
    ## 已确认发现（未达标：低于 high 或未评级，1 条）
- The summary counts them separately, and an UNRATED finding goes to "below or
  unrated" — it is not counted as low, it is not counted at all.
- The below-target findings stay in the report. They are real; they are just not
  mixed in with the ones that meet the goal.
