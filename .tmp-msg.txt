fix: the new coverage round was not in the side-quest rule

Found by SIMULATING the round sequence rather than reading it, which is what the
invariant test was supposed to be for and was not:

  r 1  recon
  r 2  generate
  r 3  generate
  r 4  coverage
  r 5  coverage      ← two in a row
  r 6  verify

Adding a RoundKind means adding it to SIDE_QUESTS, and that was missed — so
coverage could run twice consecutively, which is exactly the invariant
`tests/invariants.test.ts` exists to assert.

THE TEST MISSED IT FOR THE SAME REASON THE CODE DID: the invariant test listed the
side-quest kinds BY HAND, so a kind added later was never checked. Both lists are
now updated, and the loop tests' copy of SIDE_QUEST_KINDS too.

Worth naming as a pattern: this is the fourth time a round kind has been added or
changed and something that enumerates round kinds was not. The lists are now in
three places — loop.ts, invariants.test.ts, loop.test.ts — and only the first is
the implementation.

  r 8  consolidate · r 9 challenge · r 10 pursue · r 11 verify · r 12 pursue · …

728 tests, 0 failures.
