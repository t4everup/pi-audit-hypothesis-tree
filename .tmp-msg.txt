TWO DEFECTS, both about the banner.

1. SIX BRIEFS, TWO DIFFERENT BANNER SHAPES.

    coverage.ts   [AUDIT ROUND ${round} — COVERAGE]
    loop.ts       [AUDIT ROUND ${round} — CHALLENGE]
    loop.ts       [AUDIT ROUND ${round} — PURSUE]
    loop.ts       [AUDIT ROUND ${round} — VERIFY] / COMBINE
    recon.ts      [AUDIT ROUND 1 — RECON]                  ← hardcoded "1"
    recon.ts      [AUDIT ROUND — GENERATE from segment …]  ← NO ROUND NUMBER

`afterBanner` inserts the operator's notes and the run's scope directly below this
line because "the model uses the banner to orient itself". GENERATE is the most
common brief of all — 11 of the 51 rounds in the real Checkmk run — and it was the
one that could not say which round it was, because `renderSegmentBrief` had no
`round` parameter to print.

Both now take `round`, and `renderRoundBrief` passes it. RECON's hardcoded "1" goes
with it.

2. THE ASSERTION GATE REFUSED A CHINESE ASSERTION IT SHOULD ACCEPT.

The Chinese copula list was `是|为|属于|等于|指向|落在|进入|到达` — no `有`. So
`src/diag/ 下有一个调试端点` was refused as having "no truth value", even though it
is a factual claim that reading the directory settles. The gate's own comment says
the asymmetry is deliberate because a false REJECT is the worse error: "a false
reject means the model cannot record what it found at all, and the audit stalls on
a gate it cannot satisfy".

Measured against 14 realistic Chinese vulnerability assertions before and after:
12/14 passed, and the only remaining rejection is a genuine noun phrase with no
predicate (`…的路径穿越 #0`), which is exactly what the gate is for.
