feat: contradiction warnings — a question, never a block

Two of the fields the report rests on are typed in by the model, and BOTH are
contract gates: `severity` is what `/goal severity=high` counts, and `preAuth` is
what `requirePreAuth=1` counts. Those two words are the goal ("find a pre-auth
high-severity RCE") AND the values the model fills in — a finish line drawn by the
party being measured.

A tool cannot close that. Severity and reachability are judgements about code that
only the auditor can make. What it CAN do is notice when a recorded value sits
badly next to the recorded evidence.

  POSSIBLE CONTRADICTION — a question, not a verdict, and nothing was blocked:
    - preAuth is TRUE, but the entrypoint/technique names "admin", which is an
      authenticated surface. Is an unauthenticated request really reaching it — or
      does something in front of it (a firewall, a gateway, an entry authenticator)
      reject it first?
    - a open-redirect at critical. That is right when it IS the whole finding (an
      open redirect carrying a session token is account takeover), and inflated
      when it is a step toward one. If it is a step, record what it chains into as
      a gate and let the chain carry the severity.

TWO CHECKS, both heuristics over free text, both of which can be wrong:
  - preAuth TRUE while the entrypoint/technique names an authenticated surface
  - severity critical/high on a class that is usually a chain COMPONENT
    (open-redirect, csrf, session-fixation)

So every message is a QUESTION and nothing is ever blocked. Blocking on a
heuristic is worse than missing an inflation: a refusal that is wrong teaches the
model to write around the check instead of examining the claim, and a wrong
warning only costs a sentence. Each message also says WHEN the value is right,
because a warning that only lists the wrong case is a warning that manufactures
the wrong conclusion.

preAuth FALSE and preAuth unassessed are never questioned: they claim LESS, not
more, and only inflation is worth a sentence.
