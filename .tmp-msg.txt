feat: 是否需要身份认证 — the section answers pre-auth or post-auth, not "needs verification"

I read the earlier request as 验证 (verification) when it said 认证 (authentication).
The section is renamed and repurposed, and the property behind it is now modelled.

THE QUESTION A PRE-AUTH AUDIT ASKS, and the one the operator's goal is phrased
in. The tier says how STRONG the evidence is; this says whether the finding is
even IN SCOPE.

- `AttackVector.preAuth?: boolean` — tri-state, and the third state is the point:
    true      PRE-AUTH, an unauthenticated request reaches the sink
    false     POST-AUTH, a session is required first
    omitted   NOT ASSESSED — nobody determined it
  "Nobody determined it" and "the answer is no" are different claims, and a report
  that collapses them either inflates the finding or silently drops it. So an
  unassessed finding is NOT counted as pre-auth, and the report says so in as many
  words: **这不等于认证前**.

- `preAuth: false` is preserved through the ledger as a REAL value. Dropping a
  falsy boolean would turn a recorded "requires a session" into "unassessed" —
  exactly the inflation this guards against. A test pins it.

- The report section:
    #### 是否需要身份认证
    ```
    认证后  需要已认证会话才能到达。这是一个真实的发现，但不是认证前的——要变成
            认证前，需要在它前面接一条鉴权绕过（用 requires 把那条挂上）。
    ```
  A post-auth RCE is a real finding and not the one that was asked for; the way to
  make it pre-auth is the chain edge, which is why the section points at it.

- The summary counts the metric the goal is phrased in:
    | **认证前可达** | **1/3** |

- Contract clause `requirePreAuth` (default FALSE — a post-auth finding is still a
  finding). An unassessed finding does NOT satisfy it, and the contract detail
  says why rather than just failing.
    /loop "只找认证前 RCE" requirePreAuth=1

- `hypothesis_add` / `hypothesis_vector` accept it, and every round brief now asks
  for it in the output requirements, with the same warning: omit only if you have
  not determined it, because omitting is visible and guessing is not.
