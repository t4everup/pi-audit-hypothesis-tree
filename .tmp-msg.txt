fix: a round kind whose trigger is always true eats the schedule

Third occurrence of the same bug class. Found by reading a real audit's round
sequence:

  consolidate, verify, consolidate, verify, consolidate, verify, …

7 passes in 27 rounds, combining NOTHING (0 combination nodes produced), and the
depth round never got a slot:

  round  trigger       ids  pairKeys  singleIds
    r8    new-finding  1    0         1
    r11   new-finding  2    1         2
    r13   new-finding  3    2         3
    r17   interval     3    0         2
    r19   new-finding  4    3         3
    r21   new-finding  5    4         3
    r23   new-finding  6    5         3

  combination nodes produced: 0

CAUSE: `new-finding` fired on EVERY single confirmation. A finding is a new
combination opportunity, but a project that confirms one every other round then
gets a pass every other round — and `consolidate` sits ABOVE challenge and pursue
in the precedence chain, so the depth round was starved out entirely.

And it could never be caught by the plateau: an unproductive pass sets the stall
to 1, the verify round after it reaches a verdict and resets it to 0. The counter
oscillated 0,1,0,1 forever.

THREE FIXES:

(1) AN OPEN PURSUIT OUTRANKS A DUE COMBINATION PASS. A pursuit is BOUNDED — two
    rounds per finding, closed the moment it produces nothing — so it cannot
    starve anything by going first, and interrupting a depth lead is worse than
    deferring a pass by one round.

(2) `new-finding` needs NEW_FINDING_THRESHOLD = 2 new findings, not 1. A single
    one waits for the interval.

(3) A PASS THAT COMBINED NOTHING BACKS OFF. `AuditLoopState.consolidationStall`
    multiplies the interval by 2 per consecutive unproductive pass, capped at 12,
    and resets on a productive one — the same shape as the pursue rule ("an
    unproductive round closes the pursuit").

ALSO FIXED: `evaluateRound` judged a consolidate round by `confirmedGrew`, i.e.
whether a finding happened to be confirmed while it ran. That is the model's OTHER
work, and it made a pass that combined nothing look productive. The pass's own
output is new nodes, so it now measures `nodeCountAtStart`.

AND THE THING THAT SHOULD HAVE EXISTED AFTER THE SECOND TIME: tests/invariants.test.ts.
It runs a long loop under the condition that caused all three bugs — findings keep
being confirmed — and asserts the SHAPE of the resulting round sequence:

  - no non-verify kind takes more than 50% of any 20-round window
  - verify keeps at least 30%
  - no side quest runs twice in a row
  - consolidate does not fire on every finding (<= 25%)
  - a confirmed HIGH finding gets PURSUED
  - an OPEN pursuit is finished before a pass interrupts it
  - a loop where nothing happens still stops

VALIDATED BY REVERTING: with fixes (1)(2)(3) removed, exactly the right three
tests fail —

  (fail) the combination pass does not fire on every finding
  (fail) a confirmed HIGH finding gets PURSUED — the depth round is not starved
  (fail) an OPEN pursuit is finished before a combination pass interrupts it

A regression test that does not fail on the regression is worth nothing, so that
was checked rather than assumed.

8 new tests. 711 tests, 0 failures.
