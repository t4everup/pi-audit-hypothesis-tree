fix: the coverage gap is adaptive, not a fixed depth

A fixed depth fails in both directions, and both were measured on real trees.

DEPTH 1 collapsed `src/api` and `src/admin` into `src`, so one cited file under
src/api marked the whole src tree covered — including the eight-file src/admin
nobody had read.

DEPTH 2 (the previous fix) fails on a project where everything lives under one
wrapper. The Checkmk appliance's source is one tree at `source/rootfs/...`, so
depth 2 produced THREE groups and the report said:

  | ZERO 假设的目录 | 1 |
  - `source/.idea` — 8 个文件，零假设

while 40 of 898 files had been cited. The one "gap" it named was an IDE directory.

So the walk now DESCENDS ONLY WHILE A DIRECTORY IS TOUCHED. The moment one is
untouched it is reported and the descent stops: its children are inside the same
hole, and listing them would turn one hole into forty lines nobody reads.

The same Checkmk tree, before and after:

  before:  cited 40 of 898 (4%)   gaps: 1   → source/.idea (8 files)
  after:   cited 51 of 898 (6%) — 399 in untouched subtrees
           source/rootfs/etc/apache2/mods-available              144 files
           source/rootfs/usr/share                               132 files
           source/rootfs/etc/init.d                               40 files
           source/rootfs/usr/lib/systemd/system/sysinit.target.wants   35 files
           ...

- `CoverageReport.untouchedFiles` — how many files sit inside the untouched
  subtrees. THE headline number, and the one a fixed depth was hiding: a project
  can show one tiny "gap" and still have half its files in subtrees nobody cited.
  `coverageHeadline` now carries it.
- `groupDir` is gone; `untouchedSubtrees` builds the directory tree and descends.

AND A REMINDER THAT A GAP LIST IS A SNAPSHOT, NOT A VERDICT: between two
measurements on the live Checkmk run, `source/.idea` stopped being a gap because
the loop had actually read two files in it. Checked rather than assumed.
